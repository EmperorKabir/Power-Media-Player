7. **EQ sounds robotic/low-bitrate** — `EqualizerAudioProcessor.kt` = custom DSP (10 RBJ biquad peaking filters, series cascade, Q=1.41; NOT android.media.audiofx). Chain: stereoTransform→reverb→**EQ**→audioDelay→gain→hueAnalyser (PlaybackService.kt:634-650), 16-bit PCM path. **FILTER MATH VERIFIED CORRECT** (single +6dB = exactly +6dB at centre, flat=identity, stable, no NaN, TDF-II correct) → EQ is NOT mis-tuned/unstable. ROOT CAUSE of the artifact: **a memoryless soft-knee WAVESHAPER inside the full-band path, NO oversampling, NO input headroom, applied AFTER a gain-summing resonant cascade.** Knee (:117,128-133): `if |v|>26000 → v=sign·(26000+(|v|−26000)·0.25)` then hard-clip ±32760. **26000 ≈ −2 dBFS = a very low threshold.** The cascade adds gain with NO compensating pre-attenuation (unlike Reverb's `*0.015` input gain, ReverbAudioProcessor.kt:146) → boosted transient peaks cross the 26000 knee → the hard instantaneous non-linearity spawns high-order harmonics that **ALIAS** back in-band (no oversampling) → inharmonic metallic "robotic/low-bitrate" timbre, present the instant a boost is dialled on loud material. Numerical sim (faithful repro of the code, NOT device capture): 1kHz@20000 + one band +6dB → THD ~11%; all +6dB → 12-18%; +12dB → ~36%; broadband music peaks stayed under 26000 → so the dominant mechanism is the per-sample knee firing on TRANSIENT PEAKS, not steady-state clipping. Slider ±15dB (EqualizerViewModel.kt:24-25); presets stack multiple +5..+8dB bands. Confidence high (math correct = not the cause; degradation from the in-path soft-knee/clip + no headroom + no oversampling). NOT instrumented in DiagLog.
