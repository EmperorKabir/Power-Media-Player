Privacy Policy — Power Media Player

Last updated: 9 May 2026

Power Media Player ("the App") is an offline-first audio and video player for Android, developed as a personal/hobby project by Kabir Bhasin ("the Developer"). This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

Summary

  • The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the Developer or any third party.
  • The App has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no remote servers operated by the Developer.
  • All media metadata, playback history, equaliser settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.
  • Optional cloud integrations (Google Drive, Spotify) connect directly from your device to those providers using their official OAuth flows.

Data the App Accesses on Your Device

  • Local audio and video files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and below): used solely to scan and play your media library. File metadata never leaves your device.
  • Bluetooth devices (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): used only to display the audio output picker so you can route playback to paired or nearby A2DP/AVRCP devices. The App does not log, store, or transmit Bluetooth device information.
  • Network state and Internet (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used for streaming network media (HLS/DASH/RTSP), Chromecast discovery, and the optional cloud integrations described below.
  • Foreground service and wake lock (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, WAKE_LOCK): required to keep playback running when the screen is off or the App is in the background.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only to display the standard media playback notification with transport controls.
  • System settings (WRITE_SETTINGS): used only when you opt in to override screen brightness during video playback. Reverted on exit.

Optional Cloud Integrations

Google Drive

  • If you choose to sign in with Google, the App requests permission to access files on your Google Drive in order to stream audio and video.
  • Authentication uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 flow. The Developer never sees your Google password.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on your device using Android's secure account / token storage.
  • Drive file lists and content are streamed directly from Google to your device. They are not routed through any server operated by the Developer.
  • Sign out from within the App or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Spotify

  • If you choose to sign in with Spotify, the App requests permission to browse your library and control playback. Premium is required by Spotify for full audio playback.
  • Authentication uses Spotify's official OAuth 2.0 (Authorization Code with PKCE) flow. The Developer never sees your Spotify password.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on your device.
  • All Spotify data is exchanged directly between your device and Spotify's servers. It is not routed through any server operated by the Developer.
  • Revoke access at any time at spotify.com/account/apps.

Chromecast

  • The App uses the Google Cast SDK to discover and stream to Chromecast devices on your local network.
  • While casting local files, the App runs a small embedded HTTP relay on your phone (bound to your Wi-Fi LAN IP) so the receiver can fetch your file. The relay only serves URLs the App generated for the current cast session; it shuts down when you stop casting.
  • No Chromecast-related data is transmitted to the Developer.

OpenSubtitles (optional)

  • If you sign in with OpenSubtitles, the App searches their API for SRT subtitles matching the video filename (and, if you opt in, the video's content hash).
  • Authentication uses your OpenSubtitles email + password to obtain a per-session token. The token is stored on your device in EncryptedSharedPreferences; the password is sent only to api.opensubtitles.com over HTTPS and is not retained.
  • Search queries (filename, language code, optional moviehash) are sent to OpenSubtitles. They are not routed through any server operated by the Developer.
  • Sign out from within the App at any time, or revoke at opensubtitles.com.

Podcasts (optional)

  • You can subscribe to podcasts by RSS feed URL or via the iTunes / Apple Podcasts directory search.
  • The App fetches feeds and episode artwork directly from each podcast's host and from itunes.apple.com. No subscription data is sent to the Developer.
  • Audio episodes you choose to download are saved on your device (Movies/PowerMediaPlayer/podcasts/).

Online metadata enrichment (optional, off by default)

  • If you enable enrichment in Settings, the App can look up missing track / artist / album / year / genre fields from MusicBrainz and / or Discogs.
  • Only the existing track title and (if present) artist are sent to those services. No personal identifiers leave your device.
  • Lookup results are cached locally so the same query doesn't repeat the network call.

Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (Android 13+) / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below) — scan and play your local media library.
  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — streaming, Cast discovery, optional cloud integrations above.
  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN — list paired audio devices in the on-screen output picker.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE + FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK + WAKE_LOCK — keep playback running when the screen is off or the App is in the background.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show the standard media playback notification with transport controls and the wake-up alarm full-screen notification.
  • WRITE_SETTINGS — only when you opt in to override system brightness during video playback.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM + USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT + RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — wake-up alarms (optional feature). Alarms are scheduled locally; the boot receiver re-arms them after device restart.
  • VIBRATE — wake-up alarm vibration (when enabled).

Data Retention

  • All App data lives on your device. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data, including OAuth tokens, playback history, and preferences.

Children

  • The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
